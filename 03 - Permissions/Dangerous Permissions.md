# Dangerous Permissions

## Definition
Permissions that grant access to sensitive user data or system functions.

---

## Common Dangerous Permissions

### Data Access
- READ_CONTACTS
- READ_SMS
- READ_CALL_LOG
- READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

---

### Device Control(Look for refernces)
- CAMERA
- MICROPHONE
- LOCATION (GPS + network)

---

### Communication Access(Look for refernces)
- SEND_SMS
- MAKE_PHONE_CALLS

---

## Risk Scenarios

If abused, these can lead to:
- Identity exposure
- Location tracking
- Message interception
- Spyware behavior(Look for refernces)

---

## Real-World Insight

Most mobile malware relies on:
- Over-permissioned apps
- Social engineering prompts
- Background data access

---

## Key Concept

> The danger is not the permission itself — it is how it is used.
