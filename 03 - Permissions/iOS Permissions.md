# iOS Permissions

## Overview
iOS uses a strict permission model controlled by system-level enforcement and user prompts.

---

## Permission Characteristics

- Explicit user consent required
- Per-feature permission model
- Strong sandbox enforcement

---

## Common Permissions

- Camera access
- Microphone access
- Location services
- Photos access
- Contacts access

---

## iOS Security Model Advantage

- Apps cannot freely escalate permissions
- Entitlements are pre-defined at build time
- App Store review enforces compliance

---

## Key Insight

iOS is more restrictive than Android by design.

> Apps must declare intent early and clearly.
