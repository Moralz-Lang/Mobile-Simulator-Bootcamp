# AndroidManifest.xml Analysis

## What is the Manifest?
The AndroidManifest.xml is the **blueprint of an Android application**.

---

## What It Defines

### 1. App Identity
- Package name
- Version
- App name

---

### 2. Permissions
- Declared app permissions
- Requested system access

---

### 3. Components
- Activities
- Services
- Broadcast Receivers
- Content Providers

---

## Why It Matters

The manifest tells you:
> “What the app is allowed to do before it even runs”

---

## Security Insight

Attackers and analysts both look here first because:
- It exposes app intent
- It reveals attack surface
- It shows system interactions

---

## Key Concept

> The Manifest is the control center of app behavior declaration.
