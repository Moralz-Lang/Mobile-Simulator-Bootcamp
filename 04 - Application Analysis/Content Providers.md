# Content Providers

## What Are They?
Content Providers manage shared app data between applications.

---

## What They Control
- Contacts
- Media files
- App databases

---

## How They Work
- Provide structured access to data
- Allow controlled sharing between apps

---

## Security Perspective

If misconfigured:
- Apps may expose private data
- Unauthorized access can occur

---

## Risk Insight

- Weak permissions = data leakage
- Overexposed providers = cross-app access risks

---

## Key Concept

> Content Providers = controlled data gateway between apps
