# iOS Simulator (Xcode)

## Purpose
Simulates iOS devices for app testing and development.

---

## Features
- iPhone/iPad UI simulation
- App lifecycle testing
- UI interaction testing

---

## Security Learning Value
- Understand iOS app behavior
- Learn permission structure differences vs Android
- Observe sandboxing principles

---

## Key Insight
iOS security is heavily sandbox-driven compared to Android flexibility.

>**FUN NOTE:**

The **Apple Developer Program** is the mandatory paid membership required to publish iOS apps on the App Store, costing **$99 per year**.  This annual fee applies to both individual developers and organizations, allowing the account holder to publish **unlimited apps** under a single subscription. 

**Key Cost Breakdown:**

- **Standard Program:** **$99/year** for public distribution on the App Store. 
    
- **Enterprise Program:** **$299/year** for internal distribution of proprietary apps to employees only (not for public App Store listing). 
    
- **Commissions:** Apple takes a **30% commission** on paid app sales and in-app purchases, which reduces to **15%** for developers earning under $1 million annually (Small Business Program) or for subscriptions renewed after the first year.
